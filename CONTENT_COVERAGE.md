## Coverage method

Percentages count useful instructional topics and supplied specimen/case-study papers as auditable sections. Front matter, copyright pages, provider branding, promotional wording, repeated headers/footers, blank trackers and answer-key-only pages are not revision sections. A section is complete only when the app has usable authored coverage and, for a supplied paper, all questions and verified answers.

## Completion gate

Coverage remains below 100%. No PDF section should be marked complete merely because its title exists in the curriculum. Completion requires original revision wording, adequate flashcards and questions, definitions, calculations where applicable, verified answers, useful cross-links, working UI and successful QA.
